About the apiary

Hives under chestnut trees, a short walk from the Black Sea

[Story written by the association with the beekeeper's consent.] Ayşe keeps her colonies in the chestnut and linden forests above Akçakoca. Each harvest is delivered to the processing organisation with a signed delivery form, sampled under seal and tested by an accredited laboratory before it becomes part of a batch.

Because every lot is traced, buyers can see exactly which batches contain her honey and how each of them passed its control points.
