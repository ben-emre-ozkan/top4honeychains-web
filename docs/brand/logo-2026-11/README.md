# Project logo (2026-11-26)

The project's main logo, supplied by the owner. Both files are transparent and made for light backgrounds; never redraw,
recolour or crop their content.

- `logo-wide.png` (2167 × 725): emblem, wordmark and tagline. Used in the header, on the sign-in and landing pages, the Honey
  Story and the footers (web copies `wwwroot/img/brand/logo-wide.*` 960 px and `logo-wide-480.*`, PNG and WebP).
- `logo-normal-size.png` (1254 × 1254): the emblem only. Used where the platform showed its mark (`<honey-mark>` now renders it),
  on printed labels and as the favicon set (`favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192/512`, maskable icon;
  web copies `wwwroot/img/brand/logo-emblem.*` 512 px and `logo-emblem-128.*`).

The bee animations (`wwwroot/img/bee`: `bee-loading.svg` 3-second loop for the page loader, `bee-landing.svg` plays once on the
landing page, `bee-static.svg` when "Show animations" is off or reduced motion is set) are self-contained animated SVGs from the
owner (`Downloads\TOP4-ari-animasyonlari`), used as they are. The older drawn marks in this folder stay for reference.
