# 14. The beekeeper portal {#portal}

## What the portal is {#portal-about}

The beekeeper portal, **My beekeeper area**, is the beekeeper's own part of the platform. A beekeeper who has a mobile phone and was registered with an SMS code can get a portal account. In the portal the beekeeper reads their own apiaries, deliveries, sample jars, lab results, batches and Honey Story pages, enters the **asking price** of their honey, and gives or withdraws their own consents.

The portal shows only the signed-in beekeeper's own data. A beekeeper account has no membership in any organization and cannot open any other page of the panel. The portal is opened by the platform when it is ready; until then, beekeepers get no sign-in.

## Signing in {#portal-sign-in}

1. The beekeeper registration officer sends the sign-in details by SMS: a user name and a temporary password, valid for 72 hours.
2. The beekeeper signs in on the normal sign-in page and chooses their own password (at least 10 characters, see [Your first sign-in](#first-sign-in)).
3. If a privacy notice waits, the portal asks the beekeeper to read and confirm it first.

A forgotten password is reset with a code sent by SMS to the beekeeper's verified phone (see [Forgot your password](#password-reset)).

## The home page {#portal-home}

**Home** greets the beekeeper by name and shows what is **To do** ("… delivery(ies) without your price", "A privacy notice to read and confirm"), the number of deliveries and of batches with their honey, the **Last delivery** and the **Last lab result**. The menu has **My deliveries**, **Samples and lab**, **Batches with my honey**, **My Honey Story pages**, **My apiaries**, **Privacy and consents** and **Account**.

## Your deliveries and your price {#portal-price}

**My deliveries** lists the beekeeper's deliveries "as the plants recorded them, with their containers and seals": the date, the honey type, the amount, how the delivery was confirmed, the containers with their net weight and **Seal history**, and "corrected by the platform" when a delivery fact was corrected by two administrators (the beekeeper also gets an SMS).

**The asking price.** For each confirmed delivery the beekeeper enters an asking price per kg:

1. Enter the **Price per kg** (more than 0, at most two decimals), choose the **Currency** (TRY, EUR, USD, ARS or PLN) and, optionally, a **Note**.
2. Click **Enter price** (or **Change price**). The page asks "Save this price? Total for the delivery: …".

The price can be changed until the lot is in a finalized batch; then it is locked ("The lot is in a finalized batch; its price is locked."). Every change is a new entry; **Price history** shows them all. The price is read only by the organization that owns the lot (its organization admin and Compliance Officer) and the platform administrators; by the beekeeper's association only with the consent **My association may see my prices**. It never appears on public pages.

## Samples, batches and Honey Story pages {#portal-samples}

- **Samples and lab** shows every sample jar of the beekeeper's deliveries: container, seal, laboratory, status and the cap check at reception. "Lab values appear once the laboratory approved its report; a verdict is shown only where the limit is verified."
- **Batches with my honey** shows the batches that contain the beekeeper's honey and **My share** in them, with a link to the public page. Other beekeepers are not named.
- **My Honey Story pages** lists the public pages of the finalized batches and says how the beekeeper appears on them: "your name, with your consent" or "a neutral code, your name is not shown".
- **My apiaries** lists the apiaries with province, district, hives and position. "Only you see the exact position of your apiaries."

## Privacy and consents {#portal-privacy}

**Privacy and consents** has three parts:

1. **Your consents**: each consent is optional, and "A withdrawal takes effect at once." **Give** or **Withdraw**:
   - **My name on public pages**: without it, the Honey Story shows a neutral code instead of the name;
   - **Messages about my deliveries and results**: information messages besides the confirmation codes;
   - **My association may see my prices** (only for a member of an association);
   - **My public beekeeper page**: given through the association; it can be withdrawn here.
2. **Privacy notices you confirmed**: the notices with the date of confirmation.
3. **Who holds your data**: the data controllers of every organization that holds the beekeeper's data, with their contact for requests. Requests about personal data can also be made to the organization's Compliance Officer (see [Data-subject requests](#co-requests)).

## Account {#portal-account}

**Account** changes the **Mobile phone** (a code is sent to the new number; it is used after **Verify**), the **Language**, and the **Password**. It also has the **Show animations** switch and **Show the tour** (see [The tour and the animations](#animations)).
