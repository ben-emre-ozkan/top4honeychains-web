# 13. Compliance Officer {#role-co}

## Your daily work {#co-daily}

Every organization names at least one **Compliance Officer**: the person who supervises how the organization works with the platform. You do not enter deliveries, CCP values or lab results; you review them. You:

- accept or reject the **signed photos** that replace an SMS code, for registrations and deliveries of your organization;
- review the **sampling quality**: deliveries confirmed by photo, samples the laboratory rejected and seals found broken;
- watch the **approval mechanism**: records that wait too long for approval and approvals made under the four-eyes exemption;
- handle **data-subject requests** and consent withdrawals received outside the portal;
- keep the organization's **agreements** and its **data controller** details;
- read the organization's **audit entries**.

You never review an item you recorded yourself ("You recorded this item; another person reviews it."). In a small organization the organization admin can also be the Compliance Officer, with a second membership.

Your tasks appear in **My work** under **Review signed photos**, **Reviews** and **Data-subject requests**. The menu shows **Compliance**.

## Review a signed photo {#co-evidence}

1. In **My work** open **Review signed photos**. The item opens the registration or the delivery.
2. Open the photo and check it against the record: for a registration the identity, the registry number, the beekeeper's choices and the signature; for a delivery the containers, the weights and the seal numbers.
3. Click **Accept**, or write a **Note (required for a rejection)** and click **Reject**.

What follows:

- an accepted **registration** is verified, and the beekeeper can deliver; a rejected one withdraws its consents;
- an accepted **delivery** photo lets the lot be finalized when its CCPs are done ("Signed photo accepted; the lot can be finalized when its CCPs are done."); a rejected one keeps the lot from being finalized ("Signed photo rejected; the lot cannot be finalized.").

If you recorded the item yourself, the page says so and another person decides.

## Sampling quality {#co-quality}

**Sampling quality** lists "Deliveries confirmed by a signed photo, samples the laboratory rejected and seals found broken" (**delivery by photo**, **rejected sample**, **broken seal**). For each item click **Write a review**, choose **in order** or **finding**, write **What you checked and found** and, for a finding, the **Action taken or requested**, and click **Save**. Reviews can only be added to; they are not changed afterwards.

## Data-subject requests {#co-requests}

People can ask about their personal data: **access**, **correction**, **deletion**, **objection** or **consent withdrawal**. Requests received in the portal are handled there (a beekeeper withdraws a consent at once). Requests received outside the portal (by letter, phone or in person) are recorded here:

1. Click **Record a request**.
2. Enter the **Data subject**, the **Beekeeper's number (if a beekeeper)**, the **Request**, for a withdrawal the **Consent to withdraw**, how it was **Received by**, and **What is asked**.
3. Save. The list shows the date **Received** and the date to **Answer by**: KVKK requires an answer within 30 days. A consent withdrawal takes effect when it is recorded.
4. When you have answered, write the **Answer given** and mark the request **answered** or **refused**.

## The approval mechanism {#co-approvals}

**Approval mechanism** lists "Lot and batch CCP records, storage records (CCP 10) and finalize requests waiting for approval longer than … days, and approvals and finalizing done under the four-eyes exemption". Each line names the lot or batch, the CCP (or **Finalize**) and either "waiting since …" or "approved by the same person (four-eyes exemption)". Click it to open the record.

**Who enters and approves what** shows the rights of each role of your organization. "The platform administrator keeps this configuration; ask them for a change."

## Agreements, data controller and audit entries {#co-agreements}

- **Agreements** lists the organization's data processing agreements and supply agreements (documents uploaded on the organization page with the kind **Data processing agreement** or **Supply agreement**). **Documents of the organization** opens the organization page.
- **Data controller** shows the details named in the organization's privacy notice; **Edit on the organization page** changes them.
- **Audit entries** shows the organization's latest audit entries, read-only.

You can download the organization's evidence files: signed delivery forms, registration photos, consent documents and organization documents. Every download is written to the audit log.
