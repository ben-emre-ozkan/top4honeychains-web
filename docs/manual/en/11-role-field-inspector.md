# 11. Field inspector {#role-field-inspector}

## Your daily work {#fieldinspector-daily}

The field inspector is a technician of a beekeepers' association. With the default configuration the field inspector has **no data-entry task**: CCP 0 is done at the processing plant by its sample acceptance officer.

You can:

- open **My work** and **My organization**;
- read the control points and lab reports of the lots and batches your association takes part in;
- open the **Recall report** within your association's scope.

## When CCP 0 is given to the association {#fieldinspector-ccp0}

A platform administrator can change the responsibility for CCP 0 so that the beekeeper's association records it (**CCP responsibilities** → CCP 0 → **The association of the lot's beekeeper**, capability **Association**, entering role **Field inspector**). Then:

1. After a delivery of one of your members, the task "CCP 0 · #[lot]" appears in your **My work** under **Values to enter**.
2. Enter the values as described in [Working on a CCP page](#ccp-page) and click **Save Monitoring Logs**.
3. A quality approver of your association approves it.

The laboratory can enter results only after CCP 0 is approved.
