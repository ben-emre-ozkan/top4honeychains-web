# Interface texts shown in English in another language

Found in Phase 2 (21 November 2026) while translating the manuals and taking the pictures in tr, es, pl and lv: one row per page and label that the interface shows in English while the manual's language is another one. The manuals write these labels as the screen shows them.

| Page | Label | Language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oversight > Audit log (Gözetim > Denetim kaydı) | Hash (column heading) | tr |
| Oversight > Audit log: search | SelfApproved (marker) | tr |
| Oversight > Audit log: search | ControlSheet (marker) | tr |

Not a translation but noted: the platform's own organisation is called "Platform administration" (its name from migration 004), so this English name appears in the organisations list and the role chip of platform administrators in every language. It is one stored name; an administrator can rename it on the organisation page.

Fixed in D-96 (21 November 2026), so no longer in English:
- the beekeeper portal's "Honey Story" in es, pl and lv (menu, batches page, consent help) now uses the word of the rest of the interface: Historia de la miel, Historia miodu, Medus stāsts;
- the sample production period of the manuals' database has its name in tr, es, pl and lv (2026 Kestane Balı Hasat Sezonu, Temporada de cosecha de miel de castaño 2026, Sezon zbioru miodu kasztanowego 2026, 2026. gada kastaņu medus ievākšanas sezona); on a live server an administrator enters these names under **Set-up > Translations of reference data**.

Also corrected in D-96 (texts that were translated but wrong): lv **Open** "Atvērt", **New batch** "Jauna gala partija", the capability **Platform administration** "Platformas administrēšana"; tr the menu **Oversight** "Gözetim", custody "Muhafaza", holder "partiyi elinde bulunduran kurum", the capability **Platform administration** "Platform yönetimi".

Still open: the capability **Platform administration** is named like the person in es ("Administrador de la plataforma") and pl ("Administrator platformy"), as it was in tr and lv; in pl the word "Funkcja" is used both for capabilities and for the functions of **Role permissions**. Both are marked in `glossary.md` for the partners.
