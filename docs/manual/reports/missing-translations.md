# Interface texts shown in English in another language

Found in Phase 2 (21 November 2026) while translating the manuals and taking the pictures in tr, es, pl and lv: one row per page and label that the interface shows (fully or partly) in English while the manual's language is another one. The manuals write these labels as the screen shows them.

| Page | Label | Language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oversight > Audit log (Denetim kaydı ve hash zinciri) | Hash (column heading) | tr |
| Oversight > Audit log: search (Denetim kaydında arama yapın) | SelfApproved (marker) | tr |
| Oversight > Audit log: search (Denetim kaydında arama yapın) | ControlSheet (marker) | tr |
| Beekeeper portal, menu (Mi área de apicultor) | Mis páginas Honey Story (partly English) | es |
| Beekeeper portal, menu (Portal pszczelarza) | Moje strony Honey Story (partly English; the rest of the interface says "Historia miodu") | pl |
| Beekeeper portal > Batches with my honey (Partie z moim miodem) | Honey Story | pl |
| Beekeeper portal > Privacy and consents (Prywatność i zgody) | Honey Story (help text of "Moje imię i nazwisko na stronach publicznych") | pl |
| Beekeeper portal, start page and menu (Lasīt sākumlapu) | Manas Honey Story lapas (partly English) | lv |
| Beekeeper portal > Samples and lab (Skatīt laboratorijas rezultātus …) | Manas Honey Story lapas (partly English) | lv |
| Record a delivery, list Production period (all four languages) | 2026 Chestnut Honey Harvest Season: a production period added by the sample data in English, without a translation (reference data added after migration 010) | tr, es, pl, lv |

Also noted by the translators (texts that are translated but look wrong, listed as unsure in `glossary.md`):
- lv: the button **Open** shows "Atvērts" (a past participle, "opened"); **New batch** shows "Jauna partija" where the rest of the interface says "gala partija"; the capability **Platform administration** shows "Platformas administrators" (the person, not the capability).
- pl: the word "Funkcja" is used both for capabilities and for the functions of **Role permissions**.
- tr, es, pl, lv: some labels have different texts on different screens (for example "To" on the shipment form); the manuals follow the screen of each task.
