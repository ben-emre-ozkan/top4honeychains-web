# Interface issues found while writing the English manual

Found on 2026-11-21 in the manual's own run of the platform (fictional database, all four feature switches on). Items 2, 3, 6, 8 and 10 were fixed in D-94 and items 4, 5, 7, 9, 13, 14, 16 to 19 and the text of 15 in D-95 (21 November 2026); items 1, 11 and 12 stay as they are. The manual describes what the screen does today.

| # | Where | What happens | Effect on the manual | Proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Record a delivery, table **Containers and samples** | The table needs about 1470 px. At 1280 px (and at 1366 px) it scrolls sideways; the columns **Type** or **New seal after sampling** are out of view. | The table is shown in two pictures (left and right part). | Wrap a row onto two lines, or let the seal columns stack under the weights. |
| 2 | Beekeeper portal header on a phone | The language switch added in D-92 shows five underlined links stacked on top of each other next to the logo. | Visible in every portal picture. | Use the same compact language menu as the panel, or a single select. **Fixed in D-94** (one compact select). |
| 3 | Consumer **Send feedback to the producer** on a phone | The radio buttons of **What is it about?** sit far from their labels, at the left edge. | Visible in the feedback picture. | Align the radio button with its label. **Fixed in D-94** (radio buttons next to their labels). |
| 4 | Analyst's **My work** and the administrator's **Dashboard** | **Lab results to enter** keeps the single jars of lots whose composite report is approved (4 items in the sample data; the dashboard shows "Lab: Waiting: 4"). Known since D-91. | The analyst's My work picture shows the extra group. The text does not mention it. | Leave out jars that are part of an approved composite. **Fixed in D-95** (jars in a composite are no longer a task of their own nor counted on the dashboard). |
| 5 | Sample acceptance officer's **My work** after a delivery | **CCP 1** is listed under **Values to enter**, although the laboratory's approved report fills it in. | The manual says: CCP 1 is filled in by the laboratory, you do not enter it. | List CCP 1 only for the laboratory, or not at all until the report exists. **Fixed in D-95** (CCP 1 is no longer listed under Values to enter). |
| 6 | Lab report, limit of **Electrical Conductivity** | The limit reads "≤ 0.8 mS/cm; ≥ 0.8 mS/cm": the limits of blossom and honeydew/chestnut honey are shown together without saying which applies. | Visible in the lab report picture. | Show only the limit of the lot's honey type, or name the honey types. **Fixed in D-94** (only the limit of the lot's honey type). |
| 7 | Beekeeper portal **Privacy and consents** | **Privacy notices you confirmed** lists the same notice version twice. | Not visible in the pictures (below the fold). | Show each version once. **Fixed in D-95** (each version listed once). |
| 8 | Phone numbers in **Account** (portal, consumer) and **Profile** | Numbers are shown as `+905550000802`, without spaces, while forms show `+90 555 000 08 02`. | Visible in the account pictures. | Format the number for display. **Fixed in D-94** (shown as +90 555 000 08 02). |
| 9 | **Set-up > Organizations** | The association is tagged **Wholesaler** next to its name (it was created from an older wholesaler record). The word does not match any capability. | Visible in the organisations picture; not explained in the text. | Show the tag only to the platform administrator with a tooltip, or say "former wholesaler record". **Fixed in D-95** (the tag is gone). |
| 10 | English interface | English uses the US flag and US date format in long dates ("SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 21, 2026"), while short dates are day.month.year. The partners write British English. | Visible on the dashboard. | Use a neutral or UK flag and one date format. **Fixed in D-94** (UK flag and British dates). |
| 11 | Role chip in the top bar | Long organisation names are cut ("Akçakovan Arıcılar …"). | Visible in most pictures; the full name is in the role list. | Acceptable; or show the full name on hover. |
| 12 | Privacy notices and consent texts | All notices carry the badge **DRAFT** ("for review by the university's data-protection office"). | The badge is visible in the notice pictures. | Expected until the texts are approved; the pictures need retaking after publishing. |
| 13 | **Compliance > Data controller**, button **Privacy notice** | The button opens `/Notices`, which only platform administrators may open. A Compliance Officer gets "access denied" (checked in the manual's run). | The manual does not mention the button. | Link to the public page **Privacy and consent** (`/privacy`) of the organisation instead. **Fixed in D-95** (the button opens the public notice of the organisation). |
| 14 | **Oversight > Notices and consent texts > Publish a new version**, list **For** | The audience "consumers" has no label in any of the five resource files (`Notice.Audience.Consumer`), so the list and the notice table show the raw key. | The manual writes "consumers" without bold. | Add the label in the five languages. **Fixed in D-95** (label in five languages). |
| 15 | **Set-up > Beekeeper registry and associations** | The text at the top still says that beekeepers are records and never sign in, although the beekeeper portal gives them accounts. Any administrator can decide a registry request, also the one who submitted it. | The manual describes what happens. | Update the text; require the proof or drop the word "required"; let a second administrator decide. **Text fixed in D-95** (the intro text describes the portal and the registration). Correction: a number without a proof document is refused ("A proof document is required for manual verification."), so the word "required" was right. The second-administrator rule is left as it is. |
| 16 | **Set-up > Capabilities**, **Beekeeping enterprise** | The description says "Beekeepers have no accounts", which is no longer true with the beekeeper portal. | The manual follows the portal. | Update the description in the five languages. **Fixed in D-95** (new description in five languages, migration 031). |
| 17 | **Compliance > Audit entries** | The column **Change** shows internal record names (for example "Update · RawHoneyCCPAssignments 12/3") instead of the readable labels of the administrator's audit log. | The manual says only that the list shows the action and the record. | Use the same readable labels as **Oversight > Audit log**. **Fixed in D-95** (readable labels). |
| 18 | **Set-up > Organizations**, a capability added on the organisation page | The message "Capability … added." names the capability by its internal code, not by its name on screen. | Not shown in a picture. | Show the translated name. **Fixed in D-95** (the capability is named as on screen). |
| 19 | **Oversight > Data retention** | The period of SMS messages reads "1 years". | Visible in the retention picture. | Use the singular for 1. **Fixed in D-95** ("1 year"). |

Not issues, but noted:
- The browser tab title of the panel pages is "… | Management Information System", a name used nowhere else.
- With the test SMS provider and `Sms:DemoPanel` on (local demos), the registration and delivery forms show the SMS text with its code on the page. The manual's run switches it off, as on the live server.
